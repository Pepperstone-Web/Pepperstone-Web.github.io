About Pepperstone Login

Pepperstone Login is the access point for a client's online profile and account services. After signing in, users can navigate to their trading accounts, view live or demo account details, and select a platform associated with a particular trading account. Account access is separate from the login credentials used by some trading platforms.

How to Access Your Account

  1. Open the Pepperstone account sign-in page using your usual, trusted method.
  2. Enter the email address and password associated with your account, or use the sign-in method selected during registration.
  3. After signing in, open the Trading Accounts section.
  4. Select the Live or Demo account area as needed.
  5. Choose the platform associated with your account to continue.
Account access note: Use the official Pepperstone account portal and never share your password, verification codes or account credentials with anyone.

Pepperstone Web Platform

The Pepperstone web platform is designed for browser-based access on compatible desktop, laptop and tablet devices. It offers charting, instrument search, watchlists, market data and trading tools without requiring a separate desktop installation. Account holders can access it through their client area after signing in and selecting the appropriate trading account.

Mobile Account Access

Pepperstone's mobile app allows users to access their account on a supported mobile device. Users can sign in with their Pepperstone account credentials, view their account and use available mobile trading features. Some platform and account functions may vary by device, region or account type.

Live and Demo Trading Accounts

The account area can include live and demo trading accounts. Demo accounts use virtual funds for practice and platform familiarisation, while live accounts involve real funds and market risk. Clients can review the account type and platform details in their dashboard before continuing.

Login Problems and Troubleshooting

If you have difficulty accessing your account, these basic checks may help:

  • Confirm that you are using the correct email address and sign-in method.
  • Check your internet connection and ensure your device date and time are correct.
  • Try a private browsing window or another supported browser.
  • Clear browser cache and cookies if the sign-in page is not loading correctly.
  • Check that the trading platform and server details match your account.
  • Use the account recovery process if you cannot remember your password.

Trading Account Credentials

Some trading platforms require separate trading account information, such as an account number, password and server name. These details can be found in the account area for the relevant account. Make sure you choose the correct platform and server when connecting MT4 or MT5.

Account Security

Keep your account credentials private, use a strong unique password and take care when signing in from shared devices. Avoid unknown login pages or links, and contact the broker's support team through its official channels if you suspect unusual account activity.

Trading risk: CFDs are complex instruments and can involve a high risk of loss due to leverage. Make sure you understand how the products work and consider whether you can afford the risks before trading.

Conclusion
